Sourabh Suneja

Suneja Tech was born from a vision brought to life by a passionate computer teacher and software developer, Mr. Sourabh Suneja, in 2020. As a lifelong technology enthusiast, Sourabh was always driven to solve real-world problems through creative digital solutions.

With an intuitive problem-solving mind and a divine gift for building elegant software, he created a brand known for tackling everyday challenges through novel, affordable, and high-quality technical solutions. His unique blend of teaching experience and development expertise gives Suneja Tech its human-first, results-driven edge.
